# StorySplitter AI - Professional Multi-Page Storyboard Frame Extractor

> Split AI Storyboards Into Clean Panels Instantly. Automatically detect panel boundaries, lock frames to 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratios, edit labels in-canvas, and batch export your storyboards in seconds.
> For the complete documentation index, see [llms.txt](/llms.txt) or [llms-full.txt](/llms-full.txt).

## How StorySplitter AI Simplifies Your Workflow

1. **Import Sheet**: Drag and drop your AI-generated sheets from Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux, or pencil sketches into the browser.
2. **Auto-Detect & Crop**: Run boundary auto-detection, adjust aspect locks (16:9 for film, 9:16 for shorts), or customize grid alignment.
3. **Download Named ZIP**: Name your sequence, optionally burn labels onto images, and export all cropped panels as high-resolution PNGs or JPEGs.

## Key Features Designed for Filmmakers

- **Adaptive AI Edge Detection**: Detect borders and drawings dynamically. Our smart computer vision model learns from your corrections to recommend optimal thresholds.
- **Aspect Ratio Locking**: Instantly lock crops to standard cinema formats (16:9) or vertical smartphone formats (9:16). Or, resize panels freely.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**: Your work is completely secure. No files are uploaded to any server; everything processes locally in your browser. Works offline.
- **Session Recovery & Undo**: Export entire work sessions as a `.storysplitter` bundle. Drag it back in to resume edits with full undo/redo stack recovery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does StorySplitter AI support multi-page sheets?
Yes. You can upload multiple sheets at once. They will appear as a structured sequence in the sidebar where you can cycle through pages and add new ones anytime.

### Can I rename individual panels?
Absolutely. You can edit panel labels directly inside the canvas by double-clicking on the label badge, or modify them in the right-hand panel list.

### What image formats are supported?
We accept WebP, PNG, JPEG, and TIFF sheets. Export outputs can be selected as lossless PNG or high-quality JPEG compression.

### Is it free to use?
Yes! StorySplitter AI is a free open-source utility designed to speed up the pre-visualization workflow for creators worldwide.
