Quick Summary & Definition

StorySplitter AI is a free, serverless web application engineered for AI filmmakers and visual artists to extract, aspect-lock (16:9, 9:16), and batch-export individual panels from Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and Flux storyboard sheets. It processes 100% locally in-browser with zero server uploads and full offline security.

Key Features Designed for Filmmakers

Adaptive AI Edge Detection

Detect borders and drawings dynamically. Our smart computer vision model learns from your corrections to recommend optimal thresholds.

Aspect Ratio Locking

Instantly lock crops to standard cinema formats (16:9) or vertical smartphone formats (9:16). Or, resize panels freely.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your work is completely secure. No files are uploaded to any server; everything processes locally in your browser. Works offline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does StorySplitter AI support multi-page sheets?

Yes. You can upload multiple sheets at once. They will appear as a structured sequence in the sidebar where you can cycle through pages and add new ones anytime.

Can I rename individual panels?

Absolutely. You can edit panel labels directly inside the canvas by double-clicking on the label badge, or modify them in the right-hand panel list.

What image formats are supported?

We accept WebP, PNG, JPEG, and TIFF sheets. Export outputs can be selected as lossless PNG or high-quality JPEG compression.

Is it free to use?

Yes! StorySplitter AI is a free open-source utility designed to speed up the pre-visualization workflow for creators worldwide.
